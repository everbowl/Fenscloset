## Design critique

### Overall impression
The identity holds: Instrument Serif headings with the italic accent word, mono labels, the orange-on-stone main button, square photos, hairline rules. It already looks like one product, not a template. The biggest opportunity is **hierarchy**: on most screens two or three solid black pills sit next to the orange one, so the one thing to do is not obvious, and the quiet things (verdicts, counts, the way back) are the smallest text on the page. Second: **states**. There is no loading, error, or not-found screen anywhere, so every tap waits in silence and a failed load drops to Next's unbranded page. Third: **the little things**: 36px pills and 42px rows under the 44px rule, hover styles that stick on iPhone, sheets that pop with no motion, developer words ("mock", engine names, Railway key names, "SMS not connected yet") reaching the family.

### Visual hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: on Today, the board; correct. On a piece, two solid black full-width pills under the title, then an orange Add photo 1,100px down; wrong, the piece's photo and its one action should lead. On Shop, three solid black ways-in; the verdict (the whole point of "Should I buy it?") is the quietest line on the page.
- **Reading flow**: phone screens read top-down well until the action, which is usually below the fold (Today: Wear this ~1,100px down; confirm: Next ~1,100px down; piece: Add photo after 19 rows). Desktop two-column pages share one grid, so the right column's rows shift with the left column's height and leave gaps (Today, Shop, piece).
- **Emphasis**: solid black is used for secondary actions, destructive actions (Delete, Remove, Log out) and tags alike, so it carries no meaning. Orange is sometimes missing from the screen entirely (Shop, Looking-for) and sometimes doubled (store flow: Look it up + Add to closet).

### Consistency

| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Chips | Three styles: square outlined (check-in), square black/outlined (Together), pills (everywhere else) | One chip: `.pill-sm`, solid when selected |
| Buttons | Confirm batch is a square black Link beside a round black button; "+ Add closet" / "+ Add a measurement" / InlineAdd are ghost text; Refresh is an underlined link | Every tappable thing is a pill |
| Empty values | "Not set", "None", "Nothing special", "Not yet", "Nobody extra", "Pick a category", "Not sure", "Any" | One muted pattern |
| Long copy | Whole paragraphs in 11px mono caps (Looking-for intro, body photo tip, clean-out help, go-with explainer, rules) | `.mono` for labels only; sentences in Archivo 14px graphite |
| Dates | "FRI 30 OCT" day-month order | Tier 2 C |
| Looks | Today uses the outfit stack; Together and the Fits panel use the old 2×2 bone grid | Reuse Today's board |
| Images | Closet pieces cropped with object-cover in go-with, mirror rows, clean-out; store shots cropped and no error fallback | object-contain for cutouts; fallback for store images |
| Back | Header back plus an eyebrow "← Shop" / "← Black crew tee" link on the same screen | Header back only |
| Pending | Pending primary goes gray (looks disabled); error banners at the top while the action is at the bottom | Keep orange + aria-busy; one toast above the tab bar |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: big button label 13px white on #FF5A00 ≈ 3.1:1 (fails AA for that size); the 9px sub line at 75% opacity ≈ 2.3:1. Chevron hard-coded #6B6B6B (the gray Jeff rejected). Tier 2 B decides the text color on orange; the sub line goes to 11px full opacity regardless.
- **Touch targets**: `.pill-sm` is 36px and is the tap target almost everywhere; rows 42px; category tabs ~33px; mirror chips 36px; Fits month arrows 36px with 12px chevrons; list icons 28–40px; underline inputs 36px; "Shopping for · me ⌄" is an 18px-tall text button. Rule is 44px.
- **Text readability**: mono caps paragraphs of 4–5 lines; 11px mono for whole sentences. Fine for labels, not for reading.
- **Screen readers**: sheets have no accessible name; ticking seconds inside aria-live re-announce every second; 12 product cards × "Buy →" / "Judge it" read 24 times; section labels are `<p>` not `<h2>`; password forms have no username field for password managers; zip field opens the letter keyboard.
- **Motion**: sheets appear with no transition; hover styles stick after a tap on iPhone; no pressed state on rows, cards or calendar cells.

### What works well
- The background jobs with honest copy: looks, Shop lookups, the mirror, Looking-for ("Still looking", seconds, "you can leave and come back").
- The outfit-stack board and the closet grid; the Help grid with pictures (the best-looking screen).
- Sort and Filter in the URL with live counts and chips; the filter counts never hide siblings.
- The sheet system: native `<dialog>`, 44px close, safe areas, proper roles.
- The sign-in family: warm copy, forgot never reveals emails, invites always give a link.
- Lists editor: drag order, search past 12, protected closets, archive vs delete, delete blocked when in use.
- Privacy stated plainly where it matters (body photo sheet, receipts read once).

### Priority recommendations
1. **One primary per screen, and the quiet things louder.** Tier 2 D and E settle how secondary pills look next to orange; Tier 1 moves Delete/Remove/Log out to outlined pills, makes the Shop verdict the hero, and pins the main action to the bottom on Today, confirm and the piece page.
2. **States everywhere.** Skeleton loading for every route group, branded error and not-found pages, one toast above the tab bar for errors and saves, pending that stays orange, Try again on every dead end.
3. **The little things in one pass.** 44px everywhere via a hit-area pseudo on `.pill-sm` and taller rows, hover under `(hover: hover)`, pressed states, 200ms sheet motion, accessible names, live regions that don't tick, sentence-case for sentences, no developer words outside Admin.

- Wave 1 (bugs and dead ends) first, then Wave 2 (the system), then Wave 3, each with tests, screenshots, a push, a Railway build and a check-in.
- Tier 2 letters are built as they come in; an answer of "A1 … S1" is enough.
