## 3. The system (Fen's Closet v2)

What stays: the name, the wordmark, the family model (person switcher, minors, parents), every editable list, the privacy rules, the data, the words we already use (Today, Closet, Add, Shop, Fits, look, piece, Wear this, See it on me, Clean-out, Looking for, Together).

What changes, everywhere at once (one stylesheet, one set of parts):
- **White, not paper.** Page background white; image stages a soft gray (#f4f4f2); one ink. The orange stays the household's editable button color (Admin → Rules) and is used once per screen, on the one button that matters.
- **One typeface for reading.** Archivo for everything, sentence case; Instrument Serif for the greeting and the wordmark only. No uppercase mono anywhere a sentence lives. Sizes: 28 title, 22 section, 17 body, 15 secondary, 13 caption.
- **Round, not ruled.** 16px corners on cards and stages, pills for buttons and chips, no 1px rules as borders between everything; separation by space and tone.
- **Images first.** Cutouts on the gray stage fill the width; text sits under, never beside.
- **A tab bar like the ones in the references**: Today · Closet · + · Shop · Fits, icons with labels, the + a black circle.
- **One header**: the person switcher (avatar + name) on the left, nothing in the middle, one action on the right.
- **Every wait is a card you can leave.** A pinned card at the bottom of the screen: what is happening, how long it usually takes, and that the result is kept. The screen above it stays live.
- **Every tap answers in 100 ms.** Pressed state on every control, optimistic UI where the action cannot fail (hearts, chips), skeletons shaped like the content for anything slower than a beat.

## 5. Engineering that ships with it (what "make sure things work" means)
1. **Deploys never during use.** Before any deploy I read the last 10 minutes of http logs; if a phone is in the app, the deploy waits. A "New version ready · tap to reload" bar appears when the service worker sees a new build, so a stale page can never call a dead action.
2. **WebKit in the suite.** Every spec runs on Chromium and WebKit (iPhone 15 viewport); a sheet, dialog or flex bug on Safari fails the build.
3. **Images.** Thumbs and cutouts are content-keyed, so the proxy answers `Cache-Control: private, max-age=31536000, immutable`, the service worker caches them, every grid image is lazy with explicit size, and thumbs are 400px WebP.
4. **Mirror.** Remember that Google refuses `block_none` (one round trip saved per garment); one render check, then the fallback labeled; FASHN and Kling tried before the image-model repaint when Google fails its check; the step line says which engine and how long it usually takes.
5. **Looks.** The first look is built alone (about 10 s) and shown; the other two follow in the background.
6. **Line-by-line review** of the 19,724 hand-written lines, by area, with every finding fixed or listed (section 6 of this file, filled as it runs).

## 6. Review findings
Every hand-written line was read on 23 Nov by six reviewers, one per area; their reports are in `docs/reviews/2026-11-23/` (A auth/people/admin, B import/storage/closet, C mirror, D today/outfits/fits, E shop/profiles/lists, F shell/service worker/components). Each finding carries a severity word (breaks, unsafe, wrong, dead-end, slow, dead-code), the path that reaches it, and the fix. The fixes landed the same day, area by area, in the commits that follow the reports; what was left on purpose is said in each area's commit message.

What the reviews changed about the architecture, beyond the line fixes:
- Every outside call has a timeout (Claude 60 s, remove.bg 45 s, the bucket 60 s, store pages 12 s); nothing in the app can spin forever.
- The mirror's dressing is a job in the app shell, not a screen's state: it keeps running on any page, survives a reload, and gives up after five minutes with a real message.
- A plain dressing is the try-on alone; the image-model styling pass runs only after a tap, and a redraw is labeled as one.
- Photos are content-addressed and cached for a year; a closet's thumbnails are fetched once.
- Links the family pastes go through one guarded fetch (every hop resolved and checked against private networks).
- A deploy while someone is in the app shows "New version ready · Reload" instead of leaving dead buttons.
