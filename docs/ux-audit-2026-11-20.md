## Shipped (this branch)

### 1. Text: no italics, bigger labels, sentences in plain type
- Every italic is gone: the accent word in page titles, the serif "reason" and empty-state lines, the italic font file itself. `em` renders upright everywhere.
- The mono label went from 11px to 12px; the louder step (counters, tabs, banner titles) from 12px to 13px.
- New `.note` style (sans, 14px, sentence case, graphite) for any sentence of help. 60-odd places that set whole sentences in uppercase mono now use it: the closet's What's-needed rows, the Shop's three steps, the Looking-for page, Add clothes, the body profile, settings, Together, Help, the invite and reset pages, the mirror.
- Reasons (why a piece goes with another) are plain 16px sans, like Today's reason line already was.

### 2. "Add to a look" says so three times
Root cause of "nothing happened": the action worked, but the only signs were a small mono toast that left after 2.5 seconds and a thin tray. Now:
- The button turns into a solid "In the look ✓" pill that opens the look.
- The toast is a black bar with an "Open the look →" button and stays 6 seconds.
- The tray above the tab bar is solid black: "Look · 1 piece so far · Open the look →".
Same on the Shop, Looking-for, a piece's Go-with page and the closet's pick mode.

### 3. The closet shows a piece the moment it is added
Root cause of "never locked in the closet": for 10–40 seconds after Add there was nothing in the closet to show it was coming. Now the closet's first cards are:
- **Tagging…** cards for anything queued or processing (the store's photo, name from the product page, "On its way to the closet. A few seconds."). The page asks again every 2.5 seconds and the card becomes the real card by itself.
- **Didn't tag** cards for failures, with the reason in plain words and Try again / Remove right there.
- One **"N photos tagged · Confirm →"** card for photos waiting on the confirm page.
Shown in the owner's own closet, in the whole-closet view (no category, search or filter).

### 4. Complete the look: one panel, every slot
Was: one question at a time ("Add a bottom?", then "Add shoes?"), which read as "it only asked about bottoms". Now one panel: the piece you started with, a row for each missing slot (a bottom, shoes…) with the closet's pieces, each row optional, a tap picks and a second tap unpicks, and one pinned button "See it on me / on a mannequin" with the chosen pieces under it. The sheet's footer no longer clips on a phone (the sheet is a flex column; the body scrolls, the button stays).

### 5. Today
- Building: the board's shape breathes while the looks build, with "Building tonight's looks · 12s" and a plain sentence; after 90 seconds, "Taking longer than usual" and Try again.
- No looks: a plain "No looks yet" with the reason in a sentence.
- Weather block: no third column; condition, High/Low, rain and the zip on their own lines, so nothing wraps on a phone.
- Weather failure: "The weather didn't load." in plain words, with Change zip. Under it, the app now keeps the last good forecast for up to 24 hours when Open-Meteo times out (Railway logs show clusters of timeouts on 18 and 20 Nov), and tries a timed-out call once more before giving up. The log line is one line, not the whole error table.
- The three strips (Going out together, Something turned up, Clean-out) and the body-photo link are sentence case.

### 6. Today paints before the weather (21 Nov, Jeff's pick "A")
The weather was the one outside call on the screen, and the page waited for it before showing anything: on 20 Nov a Today load took 17 seconds and read as "site not working". Now the page shows the title, the plan, the looks and the strips at once, and the forecast cell streams in when Open-Meteo answers. While it is on its way the cell keeps its shape (the date is known, the numbers breathe), so nothing moves when it lands. A failed lookup becomes the "The weather didn't load." note, never an error page. Building looks still waits for the forecast (the engine wants it); that already ran in the background. Playwright holds the mock forecast back 1.2 seconds (`MOCK_WEATHER_SLOW_MS`) and checks that the looks are on screen while the cell is still breathing.
